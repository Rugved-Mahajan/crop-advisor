    🤖 About the model

    The app uses a Random Forest classifier. It has many decision trees, and each tree votes for a crop based on your 7 inputs. The crop with the most votes is recommended, and the confidence is the share of trees that voted for it.

    Everything runs on your device, so the app works without internet. Only the weather auto-fill needs a connection.

    Training data

    Dataset: Crop Recommendation (Kaggle). Inputs: N, P, K, temperature, humidity, pH, rainfall. Output: one of the crops below.

    Limits to keep in mind

    The accuracy is measured on held-out rows of the same dataset. Real fields are messier, so treat the result as guidance, not a guarantee. The model only knows the listed crops, and the fertilizer amounts are estimates.

    Random Forest model (100 trees). Advice is indicative; confirm with a local agronomist.
